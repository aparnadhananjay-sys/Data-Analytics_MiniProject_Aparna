Salary comparison dashboard
---------------------------
**Charts Included :-**

Clustered BarChart	-	Compare salary and bonus by department

Clustered Column Chart	-	Salary distribution with Salary band under Senior Leaders

Pie Chart		-	Department share of Average salary

Scatter Plot		-	Salary vs ProjectsCompleted

Tree Map		-	Gender Vs Salary Band

Table			-	To display Employee Rank based on Salary

Funnel Chart		-	Salary Band Vs Average Salary

Column Chart with drill down hierarchy 	-	Salary Distibution under Senior Leaders

**Slicers :-**

Department , EmploymentStatus , SeniorLeader , SalaryBand , Tenure , DiverseCategory , Projects Assigned , Projects Completed

**Strategic Insights:-**

•	Identify departments or leaders driving salary inflation.

•	Detect inequities in pay distribution across gender, tenure, or employment status.

•	Correlate compensation with productivity and retention.

•	Support HR and finance in designing fair, performance linked pay structures.

•	Provide leadership with actionable insights for workforce planning and budgeting.
